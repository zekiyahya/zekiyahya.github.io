Digital Epigraphy (SUX Markup)"Building the digital temple from the mud-bricks of antiquity."Digital Epigraphy (also referred to as SUX Markup) is a conceptual esoteric markup language. Operating at the intersection of media archaeology, web architecture, and cinematic stillness, it replaces standard HTML5 DOM nodes with extinct Sumerian cuneiform (ISO 639-3 sux).This project is not designed for functional computation. It is an act of algorithmic resistance against the hyper-active flow of surveillance capitalism. By embedding an unreadable, non-monetizable script into the web's semantic structure, it forces a meditative, melancholic stillness—a digital clay tablet carrying profound existential weight, baked permanently into the browser's memory.Architecture & SyntaxThe syntax is directly derived from authentic archaeological records, specifically the Gudea Cylinders (Cylinder A), which detail the construction of a sacred structure from the ground up.
