𒁹 THE CINEMATIC EXCAVATION
Cinema is an act of spiritual and philosophical excavation. It is the pursuit of visualizing the unmanifested, capturing the silent struggles of existence before they take form. Drawing upon Nietzschean existential weight and Tarkovskian stillness, I approach the screen as a mirror reflecting the ruins of the modern soul.

In my recent works, I delve into the "latent space" of AI, viewing it as a "digital purgatory" where unborn identities struggle for manifestation.
